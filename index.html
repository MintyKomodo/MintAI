What it does

One watch, built around you.

Two things make MintWatch different, and neither of them asks you to learn anything new.

  • MintOS arranges itself. Over a short setup it picks up your routine, then puts your faces, your apps and the numbers you glance at where you already reach. It keeps adjusting as your week changes.
  • MintAI is a companion that lives on the watch, not in a data centre. Ask it something and it acts: change a setting, start a workout, sum up your notifications, explain a health reading in plain words.
  • It answers instantly, and it still works with your phone left at home.

Everyday

Your wrist runs the things you already own

MintWatch does more than mirror your notifications. Say what you want and MintAI takes care of it, across the devices already in your life.

Your computer

Open an app, pull up a project, send a file to your laptop from your wrist. Say "open my email on my Mac" and it opens.

Your home

Kitchen light on, door locked, coffee started, the evening scene running before you have put your keys down. A word or a tap, hands free.

Your calls and music

Take a call, move a song from your headphones to the kitchen speaker, find the phone you left on the sofa. No digging in pockets.

It fits around what you already own: iPhone or Android, your headphones, your smart home. There is no new ecosystem to buy into.

Health and fitness

A coach that actually knows your body

Tell MintWatch what you are working toward and it builds the plan, then adjusts it around how your week is really going.

  • Goals, not step counts. Say "bench 200 lb by spring" and it works backward: what to lift today, what to add around it, and when to ease off because your recovery says so.
  • Show me how. Not sure of your form? It plays a 3D render of the movement right there on the dial.
  • A gentle heads up. Round the clock heart rate, sleep, temperature and stress let it notice you are run down before you feel it, so you can take the easy day.
Peace of mind

Quietly looking out for you, in the background

This is the part you should never have to think about. MintWatch reads its sensors all day and stays out of the way. If a moment ever does go wrong, it already knows what to do.

It notices

A hard fall, a car crash, a heart rhythm that is out of character. It reads the moment as it happens and can tell a real emergency apart from an ordinary bump.

It speaks up for you

If you cannot answer, MintAI calls emergency services and talks to the dispatcher, handing over where you are and what they need to know so help arrives ready.

It gets through

Deep in a canyon, underground, miles from the nearest tower. MintWatch reaches help in places a phone gives up, so you can still call for it.

Living with it

Charge it about as often as you water a plant

Set it down on the puck, pick it up later. No cable to line up, no nightly ritual, no watch that quits halfway through a hike.

  • Days, not hours. Up to 15 days in power saver, and around a week with the display always on.
  • Set it down and go. Wireless charging, so there is nothing to plug in and nothing to wear out.
  • Wear it everywhere. Rated to 15 ATM, which covers the pool, the sea and the shower without a second thought.
Privacy

Your data stays on your wrist

MintAI runs on the watch, so your health, your voice and your habits never have to leave it. Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared. Cloud features exist, and they are always yours to switch on or leave off.
