MintWatch Docs

Everything MintWatch does, in depth.

MintOS

Your personal operating system

MintOS is the watch's operating system, and no two copies of it end up the same. During a short conversational onboarding, the watch asks how you live — when you work, how you train, what you want on screen — and builds your starting layout from it.

From then on it keeps adapting. Watch faces, complications, and app placement reorganize around your actual behavior: a workout face appears when you usually train, focus tools surface during deep-work hours, and travel info takes over when you're on the move.

You can always pin things you never want moved. MintOS personalizes around you — it never fights you.

MintAI

MintAI — your companion on the watch

MintAI is your AI companion, running directly on the watch — not a thin client to a server. That means it responds instantly, works with your phone at home, and keeps what you say to it on your wrist.

It can do nearly anything on the watch: answer questions, change any setting, start workouts, set timers and reminders, summarize notifications, control your devices, and explain your health data in plain language.

For bigger questions you can optionally let it use the cloud. That's always your choice, clearly marked, and never required for core features.

Coaching

Coaching that knows your body

MintWatch tracks heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, stress, and movement around the clock, then turns that into coaching instead of charts.

It tells you when you're recovered enough to push, when to back off, how last night's sleep changes today's plan, and how your training is trending over weeks. Guidance arrives at the moment it's useful — before a workout, after a rough night — not as a wall of graphs.

It coaches focus too: quiet hours, interruption control, and nudges tuned to when you actually concentrate.

Connectivity

Connected everywhere

Wi-Fi and 5G are built in, so the watch works fully on its own — calls, messages, music, and MintAI all function with your phone at home.

It pairs with both iPhone and Android, plus your headphones and smart-home gear. MintWatch connects to what you already own instead of locking you into one brand's ecosystem.

The watch is smart about when to use each radio, choosing the most battery-efficient connection that gets the job done.

Device Control

Your wrist is the remote

Answer or place calls, switch audio between headphones and speakers, run smart-home scenes, find a misplaced phone — from the watch.

Because MintAI understands context, you can just say what you want: “movie mode,” “send this to my laptop,” “where's my phone?”

Battery

Weeks, not days

MintWatch is engineered for about 15 days of normal use per charge — with the AI on. A dedicated low-power layer handles always-on sensing, waking the main system only when there's real work to do.

Adventure mode stretches one charge up to 77 days by trimming to essentials: time, GPS breadcrumbs, health basics, and safety features.

Charging is USB-C — the same cable as the rest of your gear.

Privacy

Private by design

Your health data, voice, and habits are processed on the watch and stay there by default. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, and there's no ad profile behind your data.

Optional cloud features are opt-in, clearly labeled, and end-to-end protected with modern cryptography. Turning them off never breaks the core watch.

You can see and erase everything the watch knows about you, on the watch, at any time.

Hardware

Built tough

15 ATM water resistance — swim, shower, surf. Built-in GPS for phone-free runs and rides. A bright always-readable display, haptics, microphone, and speaker for MintAI.
