Meet Mint Technologies

We're a technology company building intelligent products that respect your privacy and enhance your daily life. Our mission is to make AI personal, private, and genuinely useful.

Our Story

Mint Technologies was founded on a simple belief: the best technology should work for you, not against you. In a world where most tech companies profit from your data, we chose a different path.

We're building products that put AI directly in your hands — no cloud dependency, no data harvesting, no subscriptions. Just smart, premium technology designed around the way people actually live.
